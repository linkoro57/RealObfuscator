Paste code or load a UTF-8 file, up to 8 MiB. A marker can be forged or removed; this check cannot prove who created a script.

What does a match mean?

The detector checks a public embedded watermark and the visible attribution header. Markers can be removed, copied or forged, so a match is evidence of a marker, not proof of authorship.
